# Every duelist's deck

Two decks per duelist. **Campaign / Free Duel** is the faithful build - their identity, theme and plan, with no staples bolted on - as dealt the first time you meet them (0 wins); a rematch swaps 2 more of its weakest cards per win. **Tournament** is the same character built to compete: twelve upgrades and the five staples every tournament player runs.
